- **M0 decisions made:** native-stable OD8 windows, early MISS/late OK expiry, integer-microsecond time, half-even millisecond rounding, expiry-before-action ordering, input-order action ties, explicit key transitions, and an oldest-unresolved-note lane approximation for stable profiles. - **M1 decisions made:** exact current-based LIF subthreshold updates; synthetic signed mV-equivalent synaptic drive with exponential decay; threshold checks at configurable ticks; spike timestamps on those ticks; exact off-grid synaptic arrival times; reset and refractory clamp; positive delays; presynaptic CSR adjacency; selective recording. The demo's numerical parameters are engineering fixtures, not measured fly values. See `docs/MODEL.md`.
- **Local plasticity fixture now implemented:** only explicitly masked CSR slots carry an effective-weight overlay and eligibility state. A pre spike leaves an exponential trace; a later post spike increments eligibility by that trace; same-tick spikes do not pair. Eligibility decays until an externally delivered scalar dopamine event applies the requested three-factor formula. The pre-trace and eligibility time constants, learning rate, bounds, and meaning of signed `D` have no fly calibration. The primitive has no DAN kinetics, eligibility clearing after reward, depression from reverse spike order, or compartment specificity; these are open model choices, not implied biological facts. See `docs/MODEL.md`.
- **Independent reward fixture now implemented:** centered hit-value utility, one global exponential expected-utility baseline (`α=0.1` by default), signed `δ = u − V̂`, and `D = gain × δ` at the judgement timestamp. The predictor has no lane, state, stimulus or action context; the signal has no feedback latency or DAN compartment. These are engineering simplifications, not claims about fly reward coding. See `docs/REWARD.md`.
- **Synthetic closed-loop fixture now implemented:** a 128-cell default (configurable 100–500) sparse circuit, 40-cell artificial time-to-contact input, seeded cue-gated motor exploration, fixed motor readout, and direct delivery of synthetic dopamine-like amplitude to selected plastic edges after a real game judgement. It did not change a judgement tier in any of the original 16-note frozen continuations or three paired exploration streams. The full update improved one seed sharply, worsened another sharply, and left a third at low-tier outcomes. Clipping dominates the harmful full update but its local reward direction remains unmeasured because the small perturbation lies on a flat/discretized behavioural regime.
