# MVP-B3 — Candidate 1 pre-training controllability

**2026-10-01 · FAIL under the predeclared B2 gate.** The selected γ4 KC→MBON05 contact weights did not control DNp42 cue-window spike count or first DOWN timing in any of the three nominal seeds. The model's MBON05 was silent in every completed arm. This is a failure of the frozen `MVP-C1` engineering model and its current interface, not evidence that the biological pathway cannot learn.

## Question, protocol and scope

The [B2 design](B2_CANDIDATE1_DESIGN.md#sole-next-stage--mvp-b3-pre-training-controllability) fixed the question and interpretation before the run. The [machine-readable B3 protocol](../configs/b3_controllability_protocol.json) fixed seeds **31001–31003**, one isolated current-position cue visible at **100,000 µs** with nominal hit at **900,000 µs**, the **1,100,000-µs** end, selected-contact factors **0.5/1/1.5**, four single-mechanism controls, and low/nominal/high autonomous-boundary levels. Every neural arm used the same frozen B2/B2.1 source graph, fixed encoder and motor readout; plasticity and task teaching were off. Diagnostic weight factors and transmission omissions were applied per arm without changing the source graph or training a decoder. The controlled KC/PAM pairing checks were separate local rule probes, not task training.

The primary gate required, in at least **2/3 nominal seeds**, strictly increasing DNp42 cue-window spike counts from low to initial to high selected weight and a first DOWN at least **1 ms earlier** at high than low, with a DOWN in all three weight arms. All three seeds also had to show signed, contact-confined KC/PAM pairing updates, and nominal background-only input could not produce a first DOWN. A missing, reversed, unchanged or background-only action fails the relevant condition. No seed, gain, sign, threshold, boundary level or mask was selected from the outcome.

**0/3 nominal seeds** met the weight-to-output condition; **3/3** had a background-only first DOWN. The independent A3 first-action reconstruction classifies each nominal baseline first DOWN as a **too-early null press**, not a successful note action. At low boundary, all three weight factors yielded no first DOWN for each seed; at high boundary, each seed's three weight factors again had identical DNp42 cue counts and first DOWN times. The low/high arms are sensitivity observations, not alternate passing conditions.

The intervention reached its intended input: summed cue-period KC→MBON05 chemical arrival amplitudes increased with the selected-weight factor in every nominal seed. For seed 31001 the sums were **5.102 / 8.881 / 12.660** in model coupling units across 0.5×/1×/1.5×, with the same strict ordering in seeds 31002 and 31003. Yet **MBON05 emitted zero spikes in all 39 arms**. Omitting selected KC→MBON05 output or all MBON05 output left nominal DNp42 spike counts and first DOWN unchanged. Removing the KC→MBON20 bypass shifted first DOWN 1–2 ms in two seeds but did not change cue DNp42 count; this small effect does not rescue the primary gate. Background-only arms had zero KC spikes but still emitted a first DOWN in all three seeds, exposing dominance of the artificial autonomous boundary at the fixed motor interface.

The six controlled pairing checks did satisfy their local rule condition: KC-before-PAM decreased and PAM-before-KC increased the selected contribution of the predeclared lowest selected KC (source ID **19083**), with no other selected pair or source graph weight changed. That verifies the local update sign and confinement in this fixture; it does not establish a usable causal route to action.

## Sole next proposed stage — B3.1 MBON05 input-transfer diagnosis

**Question:** Under the same frozen nominal seed-31001 cue, is MBON05 silence caused chiefly by the declared graded APL inhibition, or is the KC drive insufficient even when that one inhibitory route is removed? **Why next:** B3 proved that selected-weight contrasts reach MBON05 but no MBON05 spike carries them into the double-inhibitory output path; starting B4 training cannot test a pathway that is currently silent. **Hypothesis/outcomes:** If a matched APL-output-off branch makes MBON05 spike while the baseline stays silent, APL inhibition is a model-level bottleneck; if both remain silent, this particular APL explanation is rejected and the KC drive/intrinsic balance remains unresolved; if the trace or intervention cannot be attributed, the result is inconclusive.

**Intervention:** From one identical committed initial state, replay only baseline and APL→MBON05-output-off for seed 31001, recording MBON05 voltage, external/synaptic/APL contributions, source-tagged arrivals and spike times through cue end. Disable only APL's continuous current to MBON05 in the diagnostic arm. **Controls:** Keep source graph, selected weights, KC input, autonomous boundary trajectory, all other APL targets, PAM state, motor mapping, game, seed and numerical time step fixed; plasticity and exploration off. **Primary endpoint:** whether MBON05 crosses its fixed threshold and spikes before 900,000 µs in either arm, with exact trace attribution. **Secondary:** maximum voltage, arrival sums, APL current integral, DNp42 and first DOWN. **Interpretation:** A matched baseline-silent/APL-off-spiking pair supports APL suppression as the local bottleneck; two silent arms reject that single cause; mismatched starts or unreconstructable current contributions are inconclusive. **Do not:** tune gain, sign, threshold, boundary, seed, mask or motor; run B4 training; treat a model diagnosis as measured fly physiology. **Output:** raw selective trace, matched-state and current-balance audit, report, B3.1 status. **Stop** after B3.1 and propose one evidence-driven next stage.
