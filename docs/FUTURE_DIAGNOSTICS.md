## Completed weight-transplant experiment

The executed protocol used seeds **2000–2004** exactly as declared. It reconstructed their states by deterministic replay against the prior event ledger; the longitudinal run did not save portable checkpoint files.

At each seed's full state after 384 outcomes, compare three clones:

1. Its actual 384-outcome plastic weights, as the unchanged reference.
2. Its own plastic weights after 96 outcomes.
3. Its own plastic weights after 24 outcomes.

Only the 480 selected-edge effective weights were transplanted; the runner proved byte-for-byte restoration of the entire late checkpoint when those slots were restored. Late neuron state, queued arrivals, readout, eligibility, predictor, RNG and topology remained fixed. Plasticity and exploration were frozen in every branch. Both the existing common 32-note panel and fixed continuation notes 385–416 were probed. The raw ledger records utility, all timing errors and DOWN actions, displacement norms and bounds. The actual-384 reference reproduced the saved common probe exactly for all five seeds and reproduced the previously saved frozen continuation probe exactly for all five.

**Interpretation:** The observed rescues demonstrate effects of replacing late weights at fixed late states. Seed 2002's 96-outcome transplant is a clear harm, and seed 2004's 24-outcome transplant does not rescue GOOD+. This does not prove which updates caused damage or reconstruct the trajectory earlier weights would have produced. Existing queued arrivals and synaptic drive retain late history. See [the full result](WEIGHT_ROLLBACK_RESULT.md) before selecting any conditional follow-up.

The runner's state-integrity assertions and independent result audit passed. ### A8 — completed Branch A action-specific credit design-readiness audit

- **Question:** Can one fully specified, local, signed credit mechanism distinguish contributions that advance versus delay the *first* motor action, using only causal neural/action events available at learning time?
- **Why next:** Positive-RPE updates advance first crossing at outcomes 372/373/377, the old score is predominantly null-first, and three broad corrections failed. A causal credit representation is the missing type of information; another threshold or cost setting would not test it.
- **Hypothesis and possible outcomes:** A valid candidate yields opposite or separately gated credit for behaviorally different first-action timing contributions. If no causally available signal supports that distinction, the proposed design is inadequate and A8 fails. A definable signal with ambiguous timing prediction is inconclusive.
- **Intervention:** Design and lock exactly one candidate equation, event timing, selected-edge scope and state update from existing first-action and exploration traces. Do not apply it to training or production code in A8.
- **Matched controls:** Preserve the fixed game, sensory encoder, motor readout, existing topology, selected-edge mask, saved checkpoint data and causal information boundary; compare the candidate's predicted sign and attribution with the existing nonnegative-eligibility/global-D proposal at predeclared outcomes 372/373/377.
- **Primary endpoint:** An independently checkable, event-by-event definition that predicts a distinct first-action timing consequence at those checkpoints without knowing the ideal press time or future judgement at action time.
- **Secondary endpoints:** Data required to compute the signal, memory/sparse-state cost, clipping interactions and whether reward can be assigned to an action after its judgement.
- **Predeclared interpretation:** Design-ready only if the rule is deterministic, local to a documented selected-edge path, causal, signed where needed, and makes falsifiable checkpoint predictions. Failure or ambiguity is recorded before any probe/training.
- **Do not:** Tune a learning rate, choose edge masks by score, reuse the 32 former held-out seeds as final confirmation, alter the readout/game, or run training in A8.
