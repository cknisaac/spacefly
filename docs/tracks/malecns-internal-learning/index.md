# MaleCNS internal learning: Levels 1–4D

This guide covers the earlier reduced adult MaleCNS fixture through Level 4D. It uses real selected KC→MBON05 contact relationships and anatomy-selected DAN identities, with declared engineering rules for input, electrical dynamics, learning, and output. The later [EA-MVP iteration](https://github.com/cknisaac/spacefly/blob/ea-mvp-engineering-assumption-fly-learner/docs/EA_MVP_ITERATION_1_SUMMARY.md) has its own engineering assumptions and results.
