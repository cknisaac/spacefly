## Newly exposed executable-design gaps

The locked [B2 design](B2_CANDIDATE1_DESIGN.md) fixes membrane τ=20 ms and a 2-ms chemical delay, but does **not** fix the decay/kernel of arriving chemical current. The existing `SpikingSimulator` requires `tau_syn_us`; taking its historical default of 5 ms would silently add an MVP-C1 parameter. B2 also specifies independent stationary ±20% telegraph streams with **mean** 100-ms flip interval but does not fix the interval distribution or stream derivation. Fixed-period and exponential waiting laws have different continuations. The APL graded output's application within the tick likewise needs an exact executable ordering. These are engineering choices, not biological measurements. No value was chosen from performance results because no candidate neural run occurred.

My earlier statement that no further B-side decision was needed was too broad. **No new B experiment is needed**, and the minimum first-action state owner now exists, but a small **versioned B2 executable-dynamics addendum** is needed before a checkpoint can honestly claim to restore the locked candidate. It should pin the chemical-current kernel/decay, boundary interval/RNG law and APL tick update, then update the resolved design digest without rewriting historical B2 evidence. A2.2 must resume only after that addendum and the full runner are implemented.
