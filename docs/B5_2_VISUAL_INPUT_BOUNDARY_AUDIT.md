# B5.2 — MaleCNS visual-input boundary evidence audit

**2026-09-30. Result: PASS for an anatomy-backed boundary candidate; no electrical V1.1 is selected.** This is a read-only, task-independent follow-up to [B5.1](ELECTRICAL_V1_FEEDFORWARD_DRIVE_ADEQUACY.md). The [locked protocol](../configs/b5_2_visual_input_boundary_audit.json) asked whether an independently identified visual afferent in the pinned MaleCNS v1.0 parent reaches any of the 48 selected right `KCg-d` cells untouched by the present three visual sources. It prohibited simulation, gain selection, production changes, learning and task scores.

## Source and audit

The exact 107 selected KC IDs and existing source IDs **13285/13707/13874** were frozen. The [parent ledger](figures/b5_2_visual_input_boundary/parent_ledger.json) retains source rows and annotations for **1,365 direct parent IDs, 19,692 pairs and 60,502 contacts** into those KCs. Of these, 1,258 parent IDs lie outside the selected KC class. The current three sources provide **76 pairs/1,427 contacts** and reach **59/107** KCs. The other **48/107** have no direct pair from that selected boundary, although every selected KC has at least one other traced outside-KC parent. These are `MEASURED` source-graph facts, not effective-current estimates.

The data-only [partner extraction](figures/b5_2_visual_input_boundary/partner_roi_summary.json) independently counted all **3,183** individual contacts from the predeclared source-class comparison groups in the release's traced-only synapse-partner product. Pair totals, target IDs and per-contact totals were rechecked by the [independent audit](figures/b5_2_visual_input_boundary/audit.json): **PASS**, including all 19,692 parent pairs, 60,502 contacts and seven comparison groups. Parent neuron/connection and partner-file checksums were checked against the pinned import receipts. The [candidate-set ledger](figures/b5_2_visual_input_boundary/candidate_sets.json) preserves exact target IDs and source IDs. No contact-count cutoff chose a candidate; low-count contacts remain visible.

## Independent visual evidence and exact MaleCNS bodies

`LITERATURE-CONSTRAINED`: adult `KCγ-d` cells receive direct visual input through ventral accessory calyx, and the `aMe12` cell class is a documented accessory-medulla projection to these KCs. A FlyWire visual-connectome analysis identifies `aMe12` as a leading direct visual input class and reports upstream photoreceptor input; it also finds heterogeneous, sparse combinations of visual sources across individual KCs. The MaleCNS release's type annotations are cross-connectome class assignments, not physiological recordings of each MaleCNS body. Sources: [Vogt et al., 2016](https://elifesciences.org/articles/14009); [Ganguly et al., 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-49616-z); [MaleCNS v1.0 release](https://male-cns.janelia.org/); [MaleCNS annotation paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12636603/).

`MEASURED` within the pinned MaleCNS annotations and partner product: right-soma **12740** and **13190** are traced `aMe12_R`, superclass `visual_projection`, with acetylcholine transmitter consensus. They make **48 direct pairs/569 contacts** onto **37** of the 107 selected KCs, including **20** of the 48 not reached by the original three sources. The *anatomical* union is **79/107**, leaving **28** untouched by these five source IDs. The remaining four traced `aMe12_L` IDs **12211/12517/12737/532334** also contact selected right KCs. All six `aMe12` bodies together give **88 pairs/815 contacts**, reach 49 KCs, add 25 previously untouched KCs and raise the union to **84/107**. `aMe12` can project across sides in the reference connectome, so left-soma source contacts are biologically possible; they are a laterality comparator, not part of the minimum right-soma candidate.

## Limits relevant to V1.1

The source partner table's post-contact primary-region fields are coarse: for `aMe12_R`, **468/569** contacts are assigned `PLP(R)`, 62 `SCL(R)`, one `ICL(R)` and 38 central-brain unspecified. Those labels do **not** resolve ventral accessory calyx microcompartment or postsynaptic receptor location. The source cells' exact responses to a one-lane game cue, response timing, independent versus correlated activity, and effective synaptic sign/amplitude on these 107 KCs are `UNKNOWN`. ACh transmitter consensus and a class-level excitatory expectation do not turn an exact-body effect into `MEASURED`. [Ganguly et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-49616-z) explicitly report no assigned functions for the direct VPNs to the ventral accessory calyx and describe broad, mixed visual sampling. A game-cue current into `aMe12_R` would be a separately declared `ENGINEERING ASSUMPTION` boundary stimulus.

`MEASURED` source anatomy also limits interpretation downstream. The [MaleCNS MBON32 entry](https://reiserlab.github.io/celltype-explorer-drosophila-male-cns/types/MBON32_R.html) assigns only **1,129 contacts / 6.9% of its listed input** to right `KCg-d`; omitted KC classes and non-KC parents are substantial. This does **not** convert contact percentages into tonic current. [Li et al., 2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7909955/) independently describe MBON32 as an atypical output neuron with extensive non-lobe input. B5.1's direct 107-KC volley still peaks **6.417 mV** below the frozen onset and needs a diagnostic **1.748×** local effective-input multiplier; adding visual parents upstream alone cannot establish its electrical transfer.

The B5.1 visual deficit is also not explained solely by the 48-cell boundary cut: among the **59** contacted KCs, **55** needed at least a diagnostic **10×** frozen input under the existing timing. Adding `aMe12_R` could increase input to some cells, but it supplies no independent efficacy or stimulus-frequency calibration, leaves **28** without those five direct sources, and cannot justify copying the diagnostic multiplier into V1.1. The existing V1 fixed 5-ms synaptic-current decay and LIF constants are engineering values; no exact `KCg-d_R` or MBON32 transfer curve was established in the reviewed primary sources. These are `UNKNOWN` parameters, not zero-valued effects.

## Decision and stop

**B5.2 PASS, limited to anatomy-backed boundary candidacy.** A later version can *hypothesize* the addition of exact IDs **12740/13190** to the sensory boundary. That is a traceable topology and artificial-input proposal, not an installed V1.1 circuit and not evidence that the game cue recruits those neurons. The original 115-body V1 and every frozen numeric policy remain unchanged. No simulation or task probe was run.

**Numerical V1.1 readiness: NO.** The one unresolved design question is how to bound effective visual→`KCg-d` and KC→MBON32 transfer independently of B5's failed spike outcomes. Without such a constraint, choosing new currents, thresholds or synchrony would be post-hoc propagation tuning. The next single proposed stage is **B5.3, external feedforward transfer-constraint audit**: test whether primary physiological or experimentally constrained modelling measurements provide a defensible *class-specific envelope* for those two transfers. It should compare source preparation/cell class and units to the frozen V1 values, state what cannot transfer to exact MaleCNS IDs, and either define a bounded numerical hypothesis for later V1.1 design or explicitly conclude that only sensitivity-labelled engineering assumptions are possible. **Do not execute B5.3 or implement V1.1 from this B5.2 result alone.**

### One proposed next stage: B5.3 (not run)

1. **Question:** can external primary measurements or experimentally constrained models bound visual→`KCγ-d` and KC→MBON32 transfer well enough to write a falsifiable V1.1 electrical policy without using B5 outcome fitting?
2. **Why next:** B5.2 identifies a traceable visual-source expansion; B5.1 leaves both postsynaptic transfer scales unidentified.
3. **Hypotheses/outcomes:** a preparation- and class-relevant envelope exists; evidence rules out a simple numeric transfer; or source reporting is too sparse for either conclusion.
4. **Intervention:** literature/data extraction and unit conversion only, separating presynaptic spike protocol, postsynaptic subtype, measured current/conductance/voltage, adaptation and latency. If suitable direct records exist, compare them to frozen V1's declared parameters.
5. **Controls:** pin V1 config hash and B5/B5.1 records; retain exact cell IDs, anatomy, DN proxy, APL and all unknown-effect policy states.
6. **Primary endpoint:** an independently justified bounded transfer envelope for each failed stage, or an explicit documented impossibility of deriving one from reviewed primary evidence.
7. **Secondary endpoints:** subtype/preparation mismatch, natural spike synchrony, missing MBON32 input, receptor dependence and stimulus-role uncertainty.
8. **Predeclared interpretation:** a production-ready numerical hypothesis requires traceable source values, units, cell-class applicability, a stated transfer uncertainty and no B5-derived fit. Generic KC/other MBON numbers can constrain model plausibility but cannot silently become exact MaleCNS body values. Otherwise the result is an engineering sensitivity design, not physiological calibration.
9. **Do not:** simulate, tune to spikes, search game performance, activate unknown edges, change the frozen model, train, or pick a convenient value from a broad literature range.
10. **Output:** a source-by-source evidence table, explicit V1 comparison and a V1.1 readiness decision with `MEASURED`/`LITERATURE-CONSTRAINED`/`INFERRED`/`ENGINEERING ASSUMPTION`/`UNKNOWN` labels.
11. **Stop condition:** report `PASS`/`FAIL`/`INCONCLUSIVE`, update the two branch roadmaps, propose one next stage and stop before any electrical revision.
