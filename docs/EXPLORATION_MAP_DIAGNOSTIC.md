# Frozen exploration × map diagnostic — user-stopped subset

**Status:** Stopped at the user's request after complete seed triplets 2000–2004. The predeclared eight-seed protocol in `configs/exploration_map_diagnostic.json` was **not completed**: seeds 2005–2007 were not run in this diagnostic. These five-seed results are descriptive and must not be substituted for the planned cohort or an M2 gate.

## Question and branches

The previous 384-outcome study showed 55.7% GOOD+ during the final training block but only 9.8% on its frozen common panel. This experiment asks how much of that gap is due to exploration being disabled or to changing from the training map to the independent common panel.

For each seed, the original on-condition training session was replayed through outcomes 24, 96 and 384. **Every replayed training event** and checkpoint time matched the saved longitudinal ledger before a full-state clone was made. Each checkpoint received a 2×2 comparison: the same 32-note common panel or the seed's next 32 training-map notes; exploratory motor pulses either off or at the original probability of 0.002 per tick. All branches kept weights frozen and used the same neural/readout/reward state at their checkpoint. Exploration-on was run with the checkpoint RNG state plus two separately predeclared independent streams; the deterministic exploration-off branch ran once per map. The common/off branch exactly reproduced the prior frozen probe, including every judgement and DOWN action.

At outcome 384, continuation notes 385–416 were generated by continuing the study's fixed map RNG and draw order. They were **not** training outcomes or selected from probe scores. Branch outcomes never fed back into replay.

## Observed GOOD+ on the frozen 32-note probes

Each mean gives every completed seed equal weight; for exploration on, the three predeclared streams are averaged within a seed first.

| Checkpoint | Common: exploration off → on | Continuation: exploration off → on | Continuation minus common, off / on |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 24 outcomes | 80.0% → 73.8% | 80.0% → 74.8% | 0.0 / +1.0 points |
| 96 outcomes | 42.5% → 48.3% | 43.8% → 49.4% | +1.3 / +1.0 points |
| 384 outcomes | **15.6% → 25.8%** | **16.9% → 30.0%** | +1.3 / +4.2 points |

Exploration **slightly harms the strong early frozen states on average** and partially helps weak late states. At 384 it adds 10.2 points on the common panel and 13.1 on continuation notes, while adding about 25 exploratory pulses and 11 extra null DOWNs per 32-note probe. The late on mean remains at most 30.0% in this subset. Changing to continuation notes has a much smaller and inconsistent effect. In particular, seeds 2000, 2001 and 2003 score 0% at 384 without exploration on **both** maps; their exploratory streams only partly recover GOOD+.

| Seed | Common at 384: off; on streams (%) | Continuation at 384: off; on streams (%) |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 2000 | 0; 15.625, 18.75, 6.25 | 0; 25, 25, 12.5 |
| 2001 | 0; 12.5, 12.5, 21.875 | 0; 9.375, 12.5, 18.75 |
| 2002 | 78.125; 62.5, 65.625, 78.125 | 84.375; 62.5, 68.75, 84.375 |
| 2003 | 0; 15.625, 12.5, 15.625 | 0; 21.875, 25, 21.875 |
| 2004 | 0; 18.75, 9.375, 21.875 | 0; 18.75, 18.75, 25 |

Seed 2002 is a counterexample to universal collapse: its frozen 384-outcome state scores 78.1–84.4% without exploration, and exploration often hurts it. The late failure is heterogeneous across seeds.

## What this does and does not identify

The observed late gap is **not chiefly a common-panel versus training-continuation map effect** in these five seeds. Exploration contributes to some weak late states but does not restore their strong earlier autonomous performance. This leaves the state reached after further learning, including the later weight vector, action timing, fixed readout/cooldown and credit assignment, as candidate causes. This experiment does not isolate those causes. The continuation panel is one seed-specific map and the common panel is one fixed map; neither comparison estimates broad map generalization. The early stop after seeing interim results makes all five-seed averages exploratory.
