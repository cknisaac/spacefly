**Planning snapshot, 2026-09-30.** This is a conditional work timeline, not a forecast that a passing learner exists. M2 is the synthetic one-lane engineering gate; MaleCNS circuit validation is a separate branch. The original 32-seed gate failed: plasticity-on beat off in 16/32 seeds and shuffled reward in 15/32, below the declared 24/32 requirements; pooled hit timing MAE was 60.6 ms against a ≤40-ms limit. The [root-cause report](SYNTHETIC_M2_ROOT_CAUSE.md) and [future-diagnostics handoff](FUTURE_DIAGNOSTICS.md) contain the evidence. Its 2000–2031 seeds have since been used for diagnosis and cannot confirm a new rule.

The stages are dependency ordered. A successful first candidate might take about **one to two focused weeks** including audit and documentation; failed candidates add cycles with no honest upper bound. A six-hour run cannot replace Stages 1–4, and broad parameter search on old seeds would weaken the claim. The next concrete stage is **Stage 1: lock one signed, action-specific credit hypothesis and the fresh gate contract**. This timeline does not authorize launching it.

**A11 result, 2026-09-30:** [The signed perturbation gate](A11_SIGNED_PERTURBATION_INFORMATION_GATE.md) was **INCONCLUSIVE** for the combined sign-and-timing criterion. One locked independent sign realization produced both signed local first-action tags and differential motor spikes (22 versus 10 in the first 20 ms), but both mirrored patterns crossed the fixed 10-spike threshold at the same **371.325 s**. The no-pulse control crossed 66 ms later. This supplies a causal sign label in one state without proving a useful signed timing update. A-C3/A-C4 are still open; A-C5 retention, A-C6 development and A-C7 fresh held-out M2 must wait. One next **proposed, unrun** stage is A11.1 threshold-margin identifiability. M2 remains **NO**.

**A11.1 result, 2026-09-30:** [The threshold-margin test](A11_1_THRESHOLD_MARGIN_IDENTIFIABILITY.md) was **INCONCLUSIVE** under its locked criterion. The unperturbed branch already reached threshold on that next tick, so this selected intervention could detect a delay but could not advance the crossing on the 1-ms grid. No rule or readout was changed. A-C3 signed *availability* is shown in selected states, but useful credit and A-C4 timing control remain open; C5–C7 have not run. Exactly one next **proposed, unrun** stage is A11.2 dynamic-range audit. M2 remains **NO**.
