## Provenance and meaning of labels

The [source/evidence ledger](figures/b6_1_pathway_comparison/evidence_sources.json) lists the primary studies and the [MaleCNS identity ledger](figures/b6_1_pathway_comparison/malecns_identity_ledger.json) gives every compared source ID, aggregate KC→MBON candidate pair/source row, relevant DAN→KC/MBON pair, descending route and incoming-contact coverage. The [read-only extraction](../scripts/audit_b6_1_pathways.py) matched **38,157 distinct critical rows** against the pinned MaleCNS v1.0 raw traced-only product and verified raw and normalized parent hashes. This reproduces anatomy, not electrical behavior.

**MEASURED** means a source cell annotation or aggregate directed pair/contact count in this one specimen. **LITERATURE-CONSTRAINED** means a primary experiment limits a rule or cellular role in another preparation. **INFERRED** means a proposed transfer across specimen, cell subdivision or task. **ENGINEERING ASSUMPTION** means an explicitly artificial model/interface choice. **UNKNOWN** means no audited evidence establishes it. In particular, the local flat connection product lacks per-contact mushroom-body ROI: all KC→MBON pairs below are anatomical *candidates*, and the exact plastic contacts within γ1, γ2α′1 or γ5β′2a are **UNKNOWN**. The official release advertises a separate spatial syn-partner product, but it is not among the five pinned local files.

## Plasticity and teaching audit

- **γ1pedc, LITERATURE-CONSTRAINED:** Hige's 1-second odor with four 1-ms DAN pulses at 2 Hz reduced mean MBON responses from 118±8.3 to 24±7.4 spikes (n=7); odor starting 0.5 seconds after the last DAN pulse did not change the response. This supports temporally restricted depression, not an opposite potentiation rule.
- **γ2α′1, LITERATURE-CONSTRAINED:** Berry supports paired depression and later restoration of an already depressed odor trace. Handler's γ2 supplementary experiment used ISI **0 s** versus **−1.2 s**, chosen for robust calcium plasticity. Neither yields a fitted eligibility kernel or a same-cue first-action correction policy.
- **γ5β′2a, LITERATURE-CONSTRAINED:** Handler's γ5 experiment used **+0.5 s** (KC before DAN; depression, 8 MBONs/7 flies) and **−1.2 s** (DAN before KC; potentiation, 6 MBONs/6 flies), with broad drivers. The intervals are preparation-specific test conditions, not eligible numerical parameters for an 800-ms game cue or ±73-ms timing gate.

| Resolved first-action outcome | Causal teacher requirement | Current evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Early judged action | A post-action teaching event may use the resolved early outcome and remaining/recent KC state. It must show the rule and output sign delay a future first action. | **INFERRED** feedback mapping; output sign and delayed-action effect **UNKNOWN** for all three. |
| Late judged action | A resolved late event must produce a change that advances a future first action without leaking the ideal time into the encoder. | **UNKNOWN** sign and relevant temporal eligibility; none of the three papers tests this instrumental mapping. |
| Too-early null | Immediate null-press resolution must be logged and taught or deliberately rejected; it is not an osu MISS. | **ENGINEERING ASSUMPTION** to teach it. C1 omitted this pulse; no reviewed pathway paper specifies a null-action teacher. |
| No-DOWN at deadline | Only after the deadline may absence be classified; a subsequent DAN event must still identify the responsible cue and achieve a useful update. | **UNKNOWN** same-cue credit. DAN-before-next-KC timing risks changing the next cue rather than the missed one; Berry's unpaired restoration is history-dependent and can erase an existing trace. |

Teaching uses nonnegative DAN activity after resolved outcomes and cannot send a signed error as negative dopamine. The papers establish plasticity under controlled pairings, not a complete early/late/no-action policy. Choosing an electrical sign, gain, intrinsic threshold, background drive or feedback timing from C1's failed voltage/actions would violate this stage's provenance rule. Their values remain **UNKNOWN**, or would be **ENGINEERING ASSUMPTIONS** requiring explicit independent constraints in a later design.
